# Business Owner Guide

1. Finish Business Profile, pricing and FAQs using only facts you approve.
2. Choose the paid platform AI or add a BYOK key; Demo Gemini is synthetic-only.
3. Connect channels only through your verified Meta business assets.
4. Create one of five recipes, review questions/rules, run a safe sandbox test,
   then activate.
5. Watch service-window time, missing fields and the human-review queue.
6. Use Human takeover before replying manually; explicitly resume automation.
7. Record consent and opt-out accurately. Outside WhatsApp’s service window,
   only an approved opted-in template is permitted.
8. Export or import CRM data only when your role permits it. CSV import accepts
   up to 500 rows/1 MiB with display name, company, email and phone headers.

Roles:

- Owner/Admin: settings, credentials, connections and all operations.
- Operator: CRM and automation operations, not sensitive settings/connections.
- Viewer: read-only panel access.

Emergency: pause the automation, stop queued messages, take over the
conversation, capture the safe failure code, and contact the named operator.
