# Meta App Review Evidence Checklist

No live review evidence exists yet.

Required owner-supplied/approved evidence:

- legal Business Portfolio and App ownership;
- Business Verification/MFA state;
- WhatsApp Embedded Signup configuration;
- Instagram Login/OAuth configuration;
- exact redirect, webhook, privacy, terms and deletion URLs;
- requested permissions and why each is necessary;
- reviewer account/assets containing synthetic data only;
- end-to-end screencasts for connect, receive, disconnect, deletion and error
  handling;
- WhatsApp opt-in/template/payment/PIN evidence;
- Instagram Professional account and permitted entry-point evidence;
- data use, retention and deletion explanations;
- Advanced Access/App Review decisions.

The application must remain sandbox-only until the approvals and assets are
verified. A reviewer credential must never be committed or included in this
document.
